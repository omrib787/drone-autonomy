# 0006. 3S batteries

Date: 2026-09
Status: Accepted

## Context
The frame kit's 2212 920KV motors are specified for 3S with 10 × 4.5 props, and for 4S only with 8-inch props. The kit comes with 10 × 4.5 props.

## Decision
3S (11.1 V nominal), 5000 mAh, XT60.

## Consequences
- About 2.4 : 1 thrust to weight at about 1.4 kg, so it should hover near 40% throttle.
- 4S would need smaller props and would push the motors past their current rating with the stock ones.
